Author: Agent 4 (UX/UI and customer journey). Date: 2026-10-20.

## 2. Findings

### F1. Navigation is hidden behind a burger even on a 1440 px desktop
- **Location:** global header, every page.
- **Evidence (Confirmed, `ux/d2.json`):**
  - The only controls visible in the top 100 px of the desktop home page are the skip link, "Вишлист" (19x19 px), "Корзина" (19x19 px) and the burger. There are no text links such as "Каталог", "Акции" or "Контакты".
  - The phone number appears as text in the header (see `shots/komplekty_desktop.png`).
  - All 10 sections open only from a 280 px slide-out panel (`ux_burger_open_desktop.png`).
- **Customer impact:** a desktop visitor cannot see what the shop sells, or that there are two materials, without opening the menu. Every switch between categories costs two extra clicks.
- **Commercial impact:** fewer category views per session and weaker discovery of sale pages. Well-supported hypothesis.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed (behaviour). Impact is a hypothesis.
- **Recommendation:** on ≥1024 px, show a horizontal menu: Роуп · Ротанг · Акции · Доставка и оплата · Контакты. Put the categories in a dropdown or mega-menu.
- **Validation:** Yandex Metrica Webvisor/click map on the burger; compare category views per session before and after.
- **Effort:** S–M (Tilda T228/T456 menu block).

### F2. Two parallel menu trees, each with two "акции" entries, and a sale split by material that the sale pages do not keep
- **Location:** burger menu on all pages; `/salerotang`, `/saleroup`; hub tiles on `/rotang` and `/roup`.
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Top level:** the top level has 10 items: "Главная, Каталог роупа, Акции роупа, Каталог ротанга, Акции ротанга, О нас, B2B, Доставка и оплата, Гарантия и возврат, Контакты".
  - **Duplicated "акции":** tapping "Каталог роупа" expands "акции, кресла, диваны, столы обеденные, столы кофейные, шезлонги, комплекты", so "акции" appears twice for one material (`ux_burger_submenu_mobile.png`).
  - **Leaky material split:** "Акции ротанга" (`/salerotang`, 36 cards) contains 7 non-rattan items, for example "Кресло из Роупа «Бари»", "Кресло из Роупа «Милан Лаунж»", "Этажерка Лофт с HPL" and "Стол из HPL 220х100 см". 9 of the 17 cards in "Акции роупа" also appear in "Акции ротанга".
  - **Different category names per hub:**
    - Rattan hub: "Кресла и стулья", "Подвесные кресла", "Угловые диваны", "Места для хранения", "Каталог цвета", "Прайс".
    - Rope hub: "Кресла", "Столы обеденные/кофейные", "Кашпо", "Цвета роупа", "Прайс лист".
  - **Material difference not explained in the menu:** the menu never says what "роуп" is. The explanation exists only in the home block "ЧТО ВЫБРАТЬ: РОУП / РОТАНГ", 1–2 screens down.
- **Customer impact:** a buyer who thinks in terms of "I need a sofa", not "I need rope", must guess a material first. A sale shopper opens two sale pages with overlapping content.
- **Commercial impact:** more pogo-sticking. Discounts are under-exposed or seen twice. Well-supported hypothesis.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Restructure the catalogue around categories (Кресла, Диваны, Столы, Комплекты, Шезлонги, Подвесные, Хранение, Аксессуары), with material as a filter or tab.
  - Keep **one** "Акции" page, with a material filter.
  - Add a one-line explainer next to the material choice ("Роуп — мягкая верёвка, ротанг — искусственная лоза").
  - Use the same category names for both materials.
- **Validation:** card-sort or tree test with 10–15 target buyers; Metrica funnel of menu → category → product.
- **Effort:** M.

### F3. No search and no filters in the catalogue; sorting and search exist only on `/price` and two minor pages
- **Location:** all category listings (`/komplekty`, `/kreslo`, `/divan`, `/salerotang`, …).
- **Evidence (Confirmed, `ux/metrics_mobile.json`, `ux/d1.json`):**
  - **Category listings:** `/komplekty` (36 cards on page 1 + page 2), `/salerotang` (36 + page 2), `/komplektyroup` (24) and `/kreslo` (13) have no search, no filter and no sort.
  - **Where sort and search do exist:** a sort ("Порядок: по умолчанию / Цена: по возрастанию …") and a "Поиск" field exist only on `/price`, `/cvetrotanga` and the store block on `/rotang`.
  - **Search works:** on `/price`, typing "Бари" returned 6 cards, including "Copy: Кресло Бари L (роуп)".
  - **No filter by price, size, number of seats or material** anywhere.
  - **Komplekty card names are not descriptive:** sets are named by number, such as "Комплект мебели из ротанга «№23»", so the buyer cannot scan by composition or seat count.
- **Customer impact:** to find "a set for 6 under 150 000 ₽", a mobile user must scroll 19.8 screens of `/komplekty` (13 130 px), open cards one by one, and then visit page 2.
- **Commercial impact:** high for an assortment of more than 200 SKUs. Well-supported hypothesis.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Enable the Tilda Store filters (price range, category, material, seats) and sorting on every listing.
  - Expose search in the header.
  - Rename the sets with their composition, for example "Комплект «№23»: стол 180 см + 6 кресел".
- **Validation:** search usage and filter clicks in Metrica; time to first product view.
- **Effort:** S (Tilda settings) for filters and sort; M for renaming 77 sets.

### F4. `/price` is the only place with all products and with search, yet it lists every item twice and mixes in junk
- **Location:** `/price`.
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Duplicated list:** 72 cards on load; after 3 clicks on "Загрузить ещё", 116 card nodes but only **79 unique titles**. The first 36 rattan products render twice in the DOM, and two store blocks each carry their own sort and search controls.
  - **Junk items in the list:**
    - "Copy: Кресло Бари L (роуп)"
    - 4 "Юбка для Ёлки" variants, plus 2 duplicates under "Юбка для ёлки …"
    - "Этажерка Лофт с HPL" twice
  - **Small text:** on mobile, 13.9 % of the text is under 12 px, and the card prices are 10 px ("10px: 9 900").
- **Note:** whether the duplicate block is visible to the user or only one block is shown per breakpoint is **not fully confirmed**. One of the two search inputs measured as invisible (`vis=false`).
- **Customer impact:** the price list looks unmaintained, which undermines the "премиум" positioning. The 10 px prices are hard to read.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Remove the duplicate store block and the "Copy:" and seasonal items.
  - Set a minimum price font size of 14 px on mobile.
- **Validation:** recrawl and count unique cards.
- **Effort:** S.

### F5. Product page: description text 10–12 px, "Error get alias" on every product, no header when opened directly
- **Location:** every `/…/tproduct/…` URL (234 in the corpus).
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Font sizes:**
    - Mobile product page (`…-60`): 45.9 % of the visible text is under 12 px and 71.4 % is under 14 px.
    - The composition and dimensions text is 10 px on mobile ("10px: Угловой диван Вольтерра Люкс L-образной формы, 220…"; "10px: В комплект входит:").
    - On desktop, the description is 12 px with 18.6 px line height.
  - **Error text:** the raw text "Error get alias" renders under the product on all 234 product pages in the corpus. It also renders when the product is opened as a popup from the catalogue (`ux_popup_desktop.png`).
  - **No header when opened directly:** a product opened by direct URL (search engine, messenger link) has no header, menu or cart icon (`pdp_has_header: false`). The only way out is "← Назад" or "×".
  - **Mobile popup layout:** on mobile the popup shows an 8-image gallery and the price (205 800 р.) above a sticky "В корзину" bar (390x90 px). The composition and dimensions start below the first screen (`ux_popup_mobile.png`).
- **Customer impact:** dimensions are the main decision data for furniture, yet they are the least readable text on the page. The error string looks like a broken site. A visitor arriving from search cannot browse further.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Body text ≥16 px on mobile and 14–16 px on desktop.
  - Present dimensions as a structured table (Д×Ш×В, вес, нагрузка).
  - Fix or hide the alias error (Tilda store product-page template or alias setting).
  - Add the header to the product page template.
- **Validation:** recrawl; Metrica exits from product pages.
- **Effort:** S (font, header); unknown for the alias error (Tilda support).

### F6. Colour selection is a link that sends the buyer away from the product
- **Location:** "Цвет ротанга" on rattan product pages and popups.
- **Evidence (Confirmed, `ux/d2.json`):**
  - **The link:** `<a href="/cvetrotanga">`, 12 px on desktop and 10 px on mobile, tap area **63x13 px**. It opens in the **same tab** and leaves the product.
  - **The colour page:** `/cvetrotanga` lists 11 colours as store "products" (Индийское дерево … Мокко), with sort and search controls.
  - **Colour product pages:** each colour page shows English Tilda labels "More products" and "BUY NOW" plus "Error get alias".
  - **No selector:** the product has no colour selector, and the order and cart cannot record a colour.
- **Customer impact:** the user loses the product context (on a direct-URL product page there is not even a header to get back). The colour choice must be repeated to the manager by phone.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Add a Tilda product option "Цвет плетения" with swatches (and "Цвет подушек" if relevant), so the choice is passed into the cart and order.
  - Or open the swatches in a popup on the same page.
  - Remove the colour "products" from the store and the sitemap.
- **Validation:** share of orders with a colour specified; manager feedback.
- **Effort:** M.

### F7. Dimensions missing on about a quarter of product pages
- **Location:** product pages.
- **Evidence:** 60 of the 223 non-colour product pages contain no dimension pattern (N×N, "см", "Размер", "Габарит"). Examples: `divan/tproduct/283833356302-divan-garda-2h-iz-rotanga`, `divanroup/tproduct/491338626712-divan-milan-3xl-roup`, `komplekty/tproduct/898119167562-komplekt-mebeli-iz-rotanga`.
- **Confidence:** Well-supported hypothesis (regex heuristic over visible text; some may show sizes in images).
- **Recommendation:** add a mandatory size and weight block to every product.
- **Effort:** M (content).

### F8. Mobile tap targets are too small in the header, the footer and the hub tiles; horizontal overflow on `/rotang`
- **Evidence (Confirmed, on-screen elements only, `ux/m2.json`):**

| Page | Interactive elements | < 24 px in one dimension | < 44 px in one dimension |
|---|---|---|---|
| home | 50 | 18 | 32 |
| `/rotang` | 108 | 53 | 67 |
| `/komplekty` | 215 | 11 | 57 |
| `/kontakty` | 26 | 9 | 19 |
| product | 12 | 3 | 8 |

  - **Header icons:** the "Вишлист" and "Корзина" links measure **19x19 px**, although they sit inside 48 px grey circles. Whether the circle is clickable is a hypothesis: the circle seemed decorative in the DOM. The burger is 48x48 px (fine).
  - **Rotang hub tiles:** each tile has its own small "Перейти" link (for example "/shezlong 23x22").
  - **Footer links:** the footer phone link is 119x19 px and the e-mail link 111x19 px.
  - **Overflow:** `/rotang` overflows horizontally on mobile, with `scrollWidth` 415 px against a 390 px viewport. The cause is Zero-block "Перейти" buttons (`tn-elem__7935442031738592115271`, right edge 664 px). The page can be panned sideways. All other measured pages had scrollWidth = 390.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Make the whole 48 px circle the link.
  - Make each whole hub tile one link.
  - Fix the zero-block element positions for the 320–480 breakpoint.
- **Effort:** S.

### F10. Home page: no product and no price on the first screens; the page is 22 screens long
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Home has no product cards and no prices at all:** 0 price strings in `corpus/home.txt`; the measured page has no store cards.
  - **Mobile first screen** (`ux_home_mobile_first.png`): an orange "СКИДКИ ДО 70%" banner with "ПЕРЕЙТИ НА СТРАНИЦУ СПЕЦПРЕДЛОЖЕНИЙ". Once a cookie banner is added (390x126 px, which covers 19 % of the viewport until "ХОРОШО" is tapped), no furniture is visible except a cropped photo strip.
  - **Page length:** 14 601 px, or **22 mobile screens**.
  - **Category hubs:** `/rotang` and `/roup` show no products until about 5.7 screens on `/rotang` (after 12 category tiles). `/roup` shows no product cards at all (it is a tile hub plus long SEO text).
  - **Contrast with category pages:** category listings are good. The first card starts at 0.35–0.44 screens on `/komplekty`, `/kreslo` and `/komplektyroup`.
- **Customer impact:** a visitor from an ad or search lands on a discount slogan instead of furniture and prices. To reach a price they must go through the menu or the hubs.
- **Recommendation:**
  - Put 6–8 bestsellers with prices, or the category tiles with "от N ₽", in the first two screens of home.
  - Move the long SEO texts below the fold, collapsed.
- **Validation:** A/B test or before/after scroll and click maps in Metrica; CTR from home to product.
- **Effort:** M.

### F11. Odd store block on the `/rotang` hub
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**

  - The mobile full-page screenshot also shows the hub content repeated below the footer area. Cause not established: possibly a screenshot artefact.
- **Recommendation:** replace this block with real featured products, or remove it.
- **Effort:** S.

### F12. Cart feedback is inconsistent between the catalogue and the product page
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **From a catalogue card** (mobile `/kreslo`, desktop `/kreslo`): a toast "Пуф «Турин» из ротанга добавлено в корзину" appears and the header counter shows "1" (`ux_card_add_mobile.png`). Good.
  - **From a product opened by direct URL:** no toast, no header, no counter (orchestrator's `product60_after_cart_click.png`).
  - **The drawer itself** ("Ваш заказ") lacks delivery, instalment (0 % up to 12 months) and "заявка, не оплата" messaging (orchestrator).
  - **"Цена по запросу":** cards with this price still offer "В корзину" (2 on `/komplekty`, 1 each on `/komplektyroup`, `/aksessuary`, `/hranenie`).
- **Recommendation:**
  - Use the same feedback everywhere.
  - Replace "В корзину" with "Узнать цену" on items without a price.
  - Add one line in the drawer: "Это заявка, оплата после согласования · рассрочка 0 % · доставка по РФ".
- **Effort:** S.

### F13. Catalogue length and loading
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Paginated listings:** `/komplekty` and `/salerotang` paginate at 36 per page ("1 2"). The pager is the only cue that more exists, and it sits below the 36th card, at about 19 mobile screens (13 130 px page).
  - **`/price`:** uses "Загрузить ещё" (72 card nodes initially, 3 clicks to exhaust).
  - **Loading:** cards lazy-load images, and the full-page screenshots show blurred placeholders until scrolled.
  - **Load time:** the home page took 4.8 s to the `load` event in my desktop run. Catalogue pages reached `load` in 0.3 s on repeat visits with a warm cache, so these are not comparable cold-load numbers.
  - **Console:** an "ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED" error appears on every page (`mc.yandex.ru`), possibly local network.
- **Recommendation:** show "Показано 36 из N" plus filters (F3). Use "Показать ещё" instead of pages on mobile.
- **Effort:** S.

### F14. Sticky and floating elements
- **Evidence (Confirmed):**
  - **Main site:** no floating chat button. The only fixed elements are the burger bar (top right) and the cookie banner (mobile 390x126 at the bottom; desktop 340x127 at bottom left).
  - **`/promo` mobile:**
    - a full-width "ПОЗВОНИТЬ" bar (390x60 at y=604);
    - a 60x60 round widget (`t898`) at x=320, y=594, which overlaps the call bar;
    - the cookie banner (390x126 at y=538).
    - Together they cover up to 126 of 664 px (19 %) of the viewport, and the two buttons overlap each other.
  - **Product popup on mobile:** a sticky "В корзину" bar of 90 px. That is fine.
  - **Cookie banner wording:** it offers only "ХОРОШО", with no choice. For the legal angle, see the market agent's findings.
- **Recommendation:**
  - On `/promo`, separate the call bar and the chat widget, and hide the widget while the cookie banner is visible.
  - Consider one sticky "Позвонить / WhatsApp" bar on mobile across the main site, because this is a lead-generation business.
- **Effort:** S.

### F15. Suspicious sale prices
- **Evidence (Confirmed text):** on `/salerotang`, "Кокон «Комо Люкс 2х» из ротанга — 10 000р. / 79 900р." (‑87 %) and "Кресло из Роупа «Бари Лонг» 5 400р. / 24 900р." (‑78 %).
- **Assessment:** the "Комо Люкс 2х" price looks like a data-entry error. That reading is a Preliminary assumption.
- **Recommendation:** verify with the owner. Implausible discounts hurt trust and may create pricing disputes.
- **Effort:** S.

## 3. Top 5 priorities (UX view)
1. **Fix the product page** (F5, F6, F7): readable 16 px text, a structured dimensions block, a real colour option passed to the cart, a header on direct URLs, and removal of "Error get alias".
2. **Make products findable** (F3, F4): filters and sort on every listing, search in the header, descriptive set names, a cleaned `/price`.
3. **Simplify the information architecture** (F2, F1): category-first navigation with material as a filter, one "Акции" page, a visible desktop menu.
4. **Show furniture and prices on the home first screens** (F10, F11).
5. **Mobile ergonomics** (F8, F9, F14): ≥44 px tap targets for cart and wishlist, a 14 px minimum font, the `/rotang` overflow fix, and no overlapping floating buttons on `/promo`.
